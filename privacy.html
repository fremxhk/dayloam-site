Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Data collection

Dayloam does not collect your personal data. It has no developer-hosted server, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, account or sign-in.

Your data

Your habits, logs, reflections and settings remain on your devices. If you enable iCloud, Apple syncs this information through your private CloudKit database. The developer cannot access that database.

Health information is read only after you grant permission. Raw HealthKit samples are processed on your device and are not stored or transmitted by Dayloam.

Apple services

Dayloam may use Apple services including iCloud, HealthKit, StoreKit, notifications and on-device Foundation Models. Purchases and payment information are processed by Apple.
